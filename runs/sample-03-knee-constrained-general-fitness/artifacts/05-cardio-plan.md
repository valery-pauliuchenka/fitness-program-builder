# Cardio and Conditioning Plan

## Summary
General exercise information, not medical advice. Cardio is a low-impact finisher at the end of each strength session (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday), as set in the session allocation. It is brisk walking on flat ground, or easy cycling outdoors if the user prefers, with no jumping, hills or stair descents. Core cardio is 3 sessions per week and about 24-30 minutes per week over 8 weeks. Because the guidelines give 150 minutes of moderate activity per week as the adult target ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html), [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity)), optional easy walks of 15-20 minutes on non-training days are suggested to close part of the gap. The finisher stays within about 10 minutes, so each 40-minute session has about 25 minutes of strength work after a 5-minute warm-up.

## Details
Effort scale: RPE 1-10 and the talk test. RPE 3-4 means you can hold a full conversation. RPE 5-6 means you can talk in short sentences but not sing. Do not go above RPE 6.

Knee rules for every session (from the guidelines on mild front-of-knee discomfort, [RJAH NHS](https://www.rjah.nhs.uk/our-services/therapy/supported-self-care/anterior-knee-pain/)):
- Flat ground only. Avoid hills and stairs on the way down. Walk a flat loop, or use a flat cycling route.
- A knee pain rise to 2-3/10 that settles quickly is acceptable. If it goes above 4/10 or stays high, slow down or stop and use an easier option the next session.
- If the knee locks, gives way, becomes hot, red or swollen, or pain does not improve after about a month, stop and see a GP or physiotherapist. Stop for chest pain or dizziness.

Equipment: none required for walking (outdoors, or around the home). Cycling is optional and assumes the user has access to a bike; the requirements list none, so walking is the default.

### Phase 1, weeks 1-3 (Foundation)
| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Brisk walk after strength, flat | 8 min (week 1), 8 min (week 2), 10 min (week 3) | RPE 3-4, full conversation |
| Thursday | Same | same as Tuesday | RPE 3-4 |
| Saturday | Same | same as Tuesday | RPE 3-4 |
| Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun | Optional easy walk | 15 min, 1-2 days | RPE 2-3 |

Core minutes per week: 24, 24, 30.

### Phase 2, weeks 4-6 (Build)
| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Steady brisk walk or easy cycle | 10 min | RPE 4-5 |
| Thursday | Walk with intervals: 1 min brisk, 1 min easy, repeated 5 times | 10 min | brisk RPE 5-6, easy RPE 3 |
| Saturday | Steady brisk walk or easy cycle | 10 min | RPE 4-5 |
| Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun | Optional easy walk | 20 min, 1-3 days | RPE 3 |

Core minutes per week: 30.

### Phase 3, weeks 7-8 (Consolidate)
| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Steady brisk walk or easy cycle | 10 min | RPE 5 |
| Thursday | Walk with intervals: 2 min brisk, 1 min easy, repeated 3 times, plus 1 min easy | 10 min | brisk RPE 5-6, easy RPE 3 |
| Saturday | Steady brisk walk or easy cycle | 10 min | RPE 4-5 |
| Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun | Optional easy walk | 20 min, 2-3 days | RPE 3 |

Core minutes per week: 30. Reassess knee comfort in week 8 and keep the best-tolerated pattern.

Combined time check: each session is 5 min warm-up + about 25 min strength + 10 min finisher = 40 min. The optional walks are on days with no scheduled session, and are not required.

## Progression
- Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): frequency stays at 3 per week. Duration goes 8, 8, then 10 minutes. Intensity stays easy at RPE 3-4. The goal is a gradual return, as the guidelines advise for knee discomfort.
- Phase 2 (weeks 4-6): frequency 3 per week, duration 10 minutes. Intensity rises to RPE 4-5, and one session per week adds 1-minute brisk intervals.
- Phase 3 (weeks 7-8): frequency 3 per week, duration 10 minutes. Brisk intervals lengthen to 2 minutes in the Thursday session, with other sessions at RPE 4-5. Optional walks become more regular (2-3 per week).
- Change only one thing at a time (duration, then intensity). If the knee is above the pain limit, repeat the previous week. Moving from easy to moderate effort is a planning choice and is not itself a cited guideline.
