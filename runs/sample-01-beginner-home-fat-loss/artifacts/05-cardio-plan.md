# Cardio and Conditioning Plan

## Summary

- **Sessions:** 3 cardio blocks per week, one inside each 30-minute Monday, Wednesday and Friday session. Each block
  runs 10 minutes after the 20-minute strength block, so the combined session stays at 30 minutes. No cardio is
  scheduled on any other day.
- **Scheduled minutes:** 30 min/week in all 12 weeks. About 24 of those minutes are working time and the rest is an
  easy lead-in and cool-down march. Because the time is fixed, progression comes from fewer breaks, longer work
  intervals and higher effort, not from extra minutes.
- **Activities:** all low-impact and quiet enough for a flat, with one foot always on the floor. The moves are a wall
  march with high knees, the back-and-forth step, shadow boxing (hooks and straight punches) over a march, a V step
  and, in Phase 3 only, a slow mountain climber with hands on the chair. There is no running, jumping or burpees. The
  chair seat is too high for a safe beginner step-up, so the back-and-forth step is the stepping option.
- **Effort:** set by the talk test and a 0-10 effort scale (0 = sitting, 10 = all-out). Moderate is 5-6, where you
  can talk but not sing ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html)). Phase 1 stays
  light-to-moderate (3-5), Phase 2 is moderate (5-6), and Phase 3 adds short brisker bursts at 6-7 that never go
  past 7.
- **Phases:** 12 weeks in three 4-week phases (Foundation, Build, Consolidate), following the guidelines. Week 12 is
  lighter and includes a re-test.
- **Gap to the weekly guideline:** 30 scheduled min/week is a sensible start for a sedentary beginner, but it is
  below the 150-300 min/week target
  ([ODPHP](https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know)).
  An optional everyday walking ladder (see Progression) closes the gap gradually. It is encouraged, not scheduled,
  and does not count as a training session.

## Details

### How every block runs

- **Position:** after the strength block, while you are already warm. The first minute is an easy march and the last
  minute is an easy march cool-down, so the session ends with breathing settling
  ([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)).
- **Timer:** use a phone interval timer and switch move every interval in the order listed for that day.
- **Keeping it quiet:** wear trainers or work barefoot on a mat. Place your feet down rather than stamping, and keep
  one foot on the floor at all times. If it gets louder, make the step smaller, not faster.
- **Space:** every move fits in a mat-sized area. For the wall march, keep a hand on the wall. Before the mountain
  climber, put the chair against a wall so it cannot slide.
- **Effort check:** in the work intervals, say a short sentence out loud. If you can sing, speed up a little or make
  the move bigger. If you can only get a few words out, you are at vigorous effort
  ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html)), so slow down or make the move smaller
  ([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)).

### Move menu (exercise ids from the vetted exercise selection)

| Move | How to do it quietly | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Wall march with high knees (3636) | Hands on the wall, march with knees up to a comfortable height. No hop | Mon, Wed; all easy intervals |
| Back-and-forth step (3672) | Short alternating step forward and back, landing softly. Bigger range = harder | Mon, Fri |
| Shadow boxing: hooks (2271) and straight punches | March or step-touch on the spot while punching. Alternate sides | Mon (straight punches), Wed, Fri |
| V step | Step forward-out with each foot into a wide "V", then step back in ([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)) | Fri |
| Mountain climber, slow step-in, hands on the chair seat (0630) | Step each foot in and out in turn. No hopping | Fri, Phase 3 only |

### Phase 1: Foundation (weeks 1-4), light-to-moderate

Format: 1 min easy march, 8 work rounds, 1 min easy march cool-down. Each round is one work interval plus an easy
march recovery.

- Weeks 1-2: 30 s work + 30 s easy march.
- Weeks 3-4: 40 s work + 20 s easy march.

This follows the advice to start with short bouts and build up toward 10 minutes non-stop
([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)).

| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Wall march, back-and-forth step, shadow boxing straight punches over a march (rotate each round) | 10 min (wks 1-2: 4 min work; wks 3-4: about 5 min work) | Work 4-5/10 (talk easily, slightly warm); recovery 2-3/10 |
| Wed | Shadow boxing hooks over a march, wall march with high knees (rotate) | 10 min, same intervals | Work 4-5/10; recovery 2-3/10 |
| Fri | V step, back-and-forth step, shadow boxing over a march (rotate) | 10 min, same intervals | Work 4-5/10; recovery 2-3/10 |
| Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun | No scheduled cardio (optional walking only, see Progression) | none | none |

### Phase 2: Build (weeks 5-8), moderate

Format: 1 min easy march, 8 min continuous work (switch move every minute, no rest breaks), 1 min easy march
cool-down.

- Weeks 5-6: steady pace, normal range of motion.
- Weeks 7-8: a slightly quicker tempo and a bigger range (longer back-and-forth steps, higher knees in the wall march, more
  rotation in the punches).

| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Wall march, back-and-forth step, shadow boxing straight punches over a march (1 min each, repeat) | 10 min (8 min continuous work) | 5/10 in wks 5-6, 5-6/10 in wks 7-8 (talk, but not sing) |
| Wed | Shadow boxing combos (hook-hook-straight) over a march, wall march with high knees (1 min each, repeat) | 10 min (8 min continuous work) | 5/10, then 5-6/10 |
| Fri | V step, back-and-forth step, shadow boxing over a step-touch (1 min each, repeat) | 10 min (8 min continuous work) | 5/10, then 5-6/10 |
| Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun | No scheduled cardio (optional walking only) | none | none |

### Phase 3: Consolidate (weeks 9-12), moderate with short brisker bursts

Format: 1 min easy march, 8 min continuous moderate work with short brisker bursts built in, 1 min easy march
cool-down. A burst means the same move, faster and bigger, still with one foot on the floor.

- Weeks 9-10: 4 bursts of 20 s, one every 2 minutes.
- Week 11: 6 bursts of 30 s, one every 80 seconds.
- Week 12 (lighter week and re-test): Monday and Wednesday are steady 8 min at 5/10 with no bursts. Friday is the
  re-test (below).

| Day | Activity | Duration | Effort level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Wall march, back-and-forth step, shadow boxing straight punches over a march; bursts on the back-and-forth step | 10 min (8 min work) | Base 5-6/10; bursts 6-7/10 (short sentences only, never fewer than a few words) |
| Wed | Shadow boxing combos with fast punches as the bursts, wall march with high knees | 10 min (8 min work) | Base 5-6/10; bursts 6-7/10 |
| Fri | V step, slow mountain climber with hands on the chair, shadow boxing; bursts on the boxing | 10 min (8 min work) | Base 5-6/10; bursts 6-7/10 |
| Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun | No scheduled cardio (optional walking only) | none | none |

**Week 12 Friday re-test:** repeat the week 3 Friday routine (40 s work / 20 s easy march), then rate the effort and
note whether you could talk through it. A lower rating than in week 3 for the same routine shows improved fitness.
Also record whether you can do 8 minutes non-stop at 5-6/10.

## Progression

| Phase | Weeks | Duration (scheduled) | Frequency (scheduled) | Intensity | Structure change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundation | 1-2 | 3 x 10 min = 30 min/week (4 min work per block) | 3/week (Mon, Wed, Fri) | 4-5/10 work, 2-3/10 recovery | 30 s work / 30 s easy march x 8 |
| 1 Foundation | 3-4 | 30 min/week (about 5 min work per block) | 3/week | 4-5/10 | 40 s work / 20 s easy march x 8 |
| 2 Build | 5-6 | 30 min/week (8 min continuous work) | 3/week | 5/10 | Continuous, switch move each minute |
| 2 Build | 7-8 | 30 min/week (8 min continuous work) | 3/week | 5-6/10 | Quicker tempo, bigger range |
| 3 Consolidate | 9-10 | 30 min/week | 3/week | 5-6/10 base, 4 x 20 s at 6-7/10 | Bursts added |
| 3 Consolidate | 11 | 30 min/week | 3/week | 5-6/10 base, 6 x 30 s at 6-7/10 | More and longer bursts |
| 3 Consolidate | 12 | 30 min/week | 3/week | 5/10 steady; Fri re-test | Lighter week |

**Rules for moving on:**

- Move to the next step only if the current step felt no harder than its target effort and you could still talk in
  every work interval for the last 2 sessions. Otherwise repeat the step for another week. Taking it slower is fine
  ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity/features/getting-more-active-minutes.html)).
- Progress one thing at a time: first fewer breaks (Phase 1 to 2), then tempo and range (Phase 2), then short bursts
  (Phase 3). This follows the guidelines' order of duration first, then effort, within the talk test.
- If a session feels too hard, slow down or make the movements smaller
  ([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)).
- A missed session repeats the same cardio step next time rather than skipping ahead; follow the session-level
  missed-session policy of the overall program.
- Stop and seek medical advice if you have chest pain, palpitations, dizziness or feel unwell
  ([BHF](https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises)).

**Optional everyday walking ladder (not scheduled; on non-training days or as extra on training days):**

Walking briskly is a standard moderate activity
([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html)). The advice is to start slowly and add a
few minutes at a time ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity/features/getting-more-active-minutes.html)),
and bouts of any length count toward the weekly total
([ODPHP](https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know)).

| Phase | Optional walking | Effort | Weekly total incl. the 30 scheduled min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wks 1-4) | 10 min on 2 days | 3-5/10, easy chat | about 50 min |
| 2 (wks 5-8) | 15-20 min on 3 days | 5/10, brisk, talk but not sing | about 75-90 min |
| 3 (wks 9-12) | 20-30 min on 3-4 days | 5-6/10 | about 90-150 min |

Walking stays optional, so missing it never counts as a missed session. Over time it moves the weekly total toward
the 150-300 min/week guideline
([ODPHP](https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know)).

## Sources

1. Measuring Physical Activity Intensity. CDC. https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html — Supports: talk test (moderate = talk but not sing; vigorous = only a few words), 0-10 scale with moderate 5-6 and vigorous from 7-8, brisk walking as moderate activity.
2. Benefits of low impact aerobic exercises. British Heart Foundation. https://www.bhf.org.uk/informationsupport/heart-matters-magazine/activity/aerobic-exercises — Supports: starting with short bouts and building to 10 minutes of non-stop aerobic exercise, spending more time working and less resting as fitness improves, marching on the spot and V steps as gentle moves, slowing down or making movements smaller if it is too hard, stopping for chest pain, palpitations or feeling unwell.
3. Getting More Physically Active Minutes. CDC. https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity/features/getting-more-active-minutes.html — Supports: start slowly and add a few minutes at a time toward 150 min/week, walking as an easy way to add activity, brisk pace = talk but not sing.
4. Top 10 Things to Know About the Second Edition of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans. ODPHP (health.gov). https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know — Supports: 150-300 min/week of moderate activity, bouts of any length count, move more and sit less.
